# Aluminium cookware

* do **NOT** cook the following in aluminum cookware
	* *acidic* food (tomatoes, stuff with vinegar, soy sauce, ...)
	* *salty*
* these react with the cookware and dissolve small parts of aluminium in your food, which is toxic!
