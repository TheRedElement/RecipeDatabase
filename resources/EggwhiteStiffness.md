# Eggwhite stiffness

## Turning the bowl upside down

* if you can turn the bowl upside down and the eggwhite does not fall/flow out,it is considered stiff

> some modern bowls have anti-stick surfaces.
> in that case use [Visual indicator](#Visual%20indicator) instead

## Visual indicator

* beating is complete once you have *stiff peaks*
	* *soft peaks*
		* when turning whisk upside down, the peak
			* tends to fold in on itself/drop
			* does not form at all
	* *firm peaks*
		* when turning whisk upside down, the peak
			* stays upright but not pointing upward/slumps to the side
			* looks like a bird's beak
	* *stiff peaks*
		* when turning whisk upside down, the peak
			* points straight upward and stays upright
	* *overbeaten* (no peaks)
		* when turning whisk upside down, the peak
			* does not form
			* consistency is watery and grainy
