# Preservation of egg yolks

1. place **egg yolks** in small container that can be closed airtight
2. gently cover **egg yolks** with *cold* water
	1. warm water can slightly cook them
3. close as tightly as possible
4. place in *main compartment* of fridge
	5. not in door because temperature fluctuates there

## Notes
* **egg yolks** usable for up to $4\,\mathrm{days}$ post preservation
