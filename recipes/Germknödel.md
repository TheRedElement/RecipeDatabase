# Germknödel

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (wheat)|350 g|
|milk|160 mL|
|powidl|60 g|
|sugar|60 g|
|butter|35 g|
|salt|5 g|
|poppy seeds|3 tbsp|
|sugar (icing)|3 tbsp|
|egg|1|
|yeast (dry)|according to instructions|

## Recipe

### Poppy Seeds Sugar Mixture
1. add **sugar (icing)**, **poppy seeds** to glass
2. shake until well-mixed


### Dough
1. add **flour** to bowl
2. make a small valley in the center
	1. add $\pu{80mL}$ [handwarm](handwarm.md) milk
	2. add **yeast**
	3. add part of **sugar**
	4. mix until you get a liquid pre-dough (inside the valley)
	5. sprinkle with **flour** until surface slightly covered 
3. cover with cloth and let rest for $\pu{20min}$ at warm location
	1. until visibly increased in volume and sprinkled flour shows slight cracks
	2. I often use the oven without turning it on
4. add **sugar**, **salt**, $\pu{80mL}$ **milk**, egg
5. mix well until dough starts to feel like it is binding
6. add **butter**
7. knead until smooth dough emerges
8. shape into sphere
9. cover with cloth and let rest for $\pu{60min}$ at warm location
	1. stop once doubled in size
	2. make sure dough does not rest for too long (collapses slightly)

### Filling
1. divide [Dough](#Dough) into $\pu{4-6}$ equal-sized parts
2. flatten them into $\pu{1cm}$ thick circles
3. place $\pu{1-2tbsp}$ **powidl** in the center of each of the discs
4. close the discs to spheres
	1. **powidl** should be in the center
	2. make sure there are no holes
5. place on floured surface
6. let rest for $\pu{45min}$

### Cooking the Knödel

#### Steaming
1. boil **water** in a pot
2. add a steaming-tray
3. place the filled Knödel on **parchment paper** and onto the steaming-tray
	1. **parchment paper** ensures that they don't stick on the backing tray
4. steam for $\pu{15min}$

#### Cooking
1. boil a pot of **salted** **water**
2. reduce temperature  until water is boiling but **NOT** (or only slightly) bubbling
3. carefully place the filled Knödel into the water
4. let sit for $\pu{7min}$
5. flip
6. let sit for $\pu{7min}$

### Serving
1. melt a little bit of **butter**
2. pour **butter** over cooked Knödel
3. sprinkle with good amount of [Poppy Seeds Sugar Mixture](#Poppy%20Seeds%20Sugar%20Mixture)
4. enjoy!


## Notes
* alternatively to [Poppy Seeds Sugar Mixture](#Poppy%20Seeds%20Sugar%20Mixture) you can serve the [Germknödel](Germknödel.md) with [Vanillesauce](Vanillesauce.md)
