# Mürbteig

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour|230 g|
|butter|100 g|
|sugar (icing)|30 g|
|vanilla sugar|8 g|
|egg|1|


## Recipe

### Abgebröselt

1. dice **butter** in small pieces
2. add **flour**, **butter** to a bowl or cutting board
3. rub together with your hands until you have equal-sized crumbs (bröseln, crumbling)
4. add **sugar (icing)** and continue crumbling
5. create a opening in the center of your baking mass
6. add **egg yolk**, **vanilla sugar** to the center
7. knead into a dough
8. add any other ingredients you want to include
	1. cinnamon
	2. salt
	3. spices
	4. ...
9. knead into dough
10. wrap in cling-wrap
11. place in fridge for $\approx 60\,\mathrm{min}$
