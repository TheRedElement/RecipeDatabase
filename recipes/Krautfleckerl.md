# Name Of Dish

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|pasta |300 g|
|garlic |4 cloves|
|cabbage |1|
|onion |1|
|caraway |0|
|oil |0|
|parsley |0.|
|pepper |0|
|salt |0|
|sugar |0|

- **cabbage** is medium sized
- use white cabbage
- **pasta** is supposed to be "fleckerl"
	- a noodle type from Austria
	- square flat pieces

## Recipe

1. cut **cabbage** in half and remove stem#
2. cut **cabbage** into $2\,\mathrm{cm}\times 2\,\mathrm{cm}$ pieces
	1. about the size of **pasta**
3. cook [Pasta](Pasta.md) in a large pot
4. finely chop **onion** and **garlic**
5. move **pasta** to the side
6. reusing the same pot
	1. heat **oil**
	2. roast **onion** until nice and brown
	3. add **garlic**
	4. keep roasting for a bit
	5. add **cabbage**, **caraway**, **salt**, **pepper**
	6. add a little bit of **sugar** (to caramelise)
	7. roast until **cabbage** has taken on color
7. add **pasta**
8. let sit on low heat for for $5\,\mathrm{min}$
	1. so the aromas can combine
9. season to taste
10. serve garnished with **parsley**
