# Wespennester

|Ingredient|Amount (40 cookies)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|almonds|200 g|
|sugar (icing)|200 g|
|chocolate|100 g|
|vanilla sugar|8 g|
|egg|4|
|sugar|1 tbsp|
|cinnamon|1 pinch|
|water|1 tbsp|

## Recipe

### Almond brittle

1. roughly chop **almonds** to splices
2. using a *coated pan*
3. melt **sugar** until light-brown
4. take from stove
5. add **almond** splices
6. mix
7. add **water**
8. mix
9. place on *parchment paper* for solidifying
10. once solid and dry
	1. crush into prittle

### Dough

1. extract **egg whites**
2. grate **chocolate**
3. whisk until very stiff ([EggwhiteStiffness](../resources/EggwhiteStiffness.md))
4. mix in **sugar (icing)**, **vanilla sugar**, **cinnamon**
5. fold in [Almond brittle](#Almond%20brittle) and **chocolate**

### Baking

1. preheat oven to $140\mathrm{^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. place small piles of [Dough](#Dough) onto baking tray
3. bake on central rack for $15-18\,\mathrm{min}$
4. take out of the oven and let dry for $\approx 2\,\mathrm{h}$
	1. if you just bake a single batch, you can slightly open the oven and let them dry in the oven for $\approx 1.5\,\mathrm{h}$
5. let fully cool down before placing in container
