# Kärntner Kasnudl 

|Ingredient|Amount (16 pieces)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|curd |500 g|
|potato |500 g|
|flour (wheat) |250 g|
|water |100 mL|
|butter |50 g|
|onion |50 g|0.25 onions|
|egg |1|
|garlic |1 cloves|
|chervil |0.5 tsp|
|marjoram |0.5 tsp|
|noodle mint |0.5 tsp|
|parsley |0.5 tsp|
|chives |0|
|salt |0|

## Recipe

1. prepare [GefuellteNudl_Teig](GefuellteNudl_Teig.md)
	1. follow steps until [Base](GefuellteNudl_Teig.md#Base) is completed

### The filling

1. finely dice **onions**
2. finely dice **garlic**
3. chop **chervil**, **noodle mint**, **parsley**, **marjoram**
4. cook **potatoes**
5. peel **potatoes**
6. put **potatoes** through potato-press (or mash them any other convenient way)
7. heat **butter**
8. roast **onions**, **garlic**
9. turn off heat
10. to **potatoes** add **onions**, **garlic**, **curd**, **salt**, **chervil**, **noodle mint**, **parsley**, **marjoram**
11. knead until well-combined
12. form small balls of the filling

### Combining

1. use [The filling](#The%20filling) for [Filling](GefuellteNudl_Teig.md#Filling)
2. continue from there with [Cooking](GefuellteNudl_Teig.md#Cooking)
	1. use **chives** as garnish in the end

## Preservation
- freeze while still raw
- when ready to eat
	- throw frozen [Kasnudl](Kasnudl.md) into boiling water
	- try to make sure that they don't burst

## Notes
- **curd** (Bröseltopfen) is a typical Austrian/German product
- see [Setz et al. (2006)](../references/references.pdf) for details
- also parmesan is sometimes used as garnish
