# Chocolate chip cookies

|Ingredient|Amount (26 cookies)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (wheat)|530 g|
|sugar|440 g|
|chocolate chips|425 g|
|butter|350 g|
|egg|4|
|vanilla extract|4 tsp|
|salt|2 tsp|
|baking soda|1 tsp|

## Recipe
1. preheat oven ([Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)) to $\pu{180^\circ C}$
	1. place oven shelf in the middle position
2. place parchment paper on baking tray(s)
3. extract **yolk** from $\pu{2}$ of the **eggs**

### Flour mixture
1. whisk **flour**, **salt**, **baking soda**

### Cookie dough
1. heat butter until almost completely melted
	1. this ensures that the cookies stay soft
2. whisk to finish melting **butter**
3. add **sugar**
	1. whisk vigorously for $\pu{15s}$
4. add **yolks**, **egg**, **vanilla extract**
5. whisk well for $\pu{15s}$
6. add [Flour mixture](#Flour%20mixture)
7. mix until almost fully incorporated
8. add **chocolate chips**
	1. stir until evenly distributed and **flour** fully incorporated
	2. batter should be quite loose
9. let sit for $\pu{5min}$
	1. such that batter firms up and is scoopable
	2. batter should stay in mound shape when dropped on tray

### Cookie shaping
1. scoop $\pu{1.5tbsp}$ and place on baking tray
2. separate cookies by $\approx \pu{5cm}$
3. top with remaining **chocolate chips**

### Baking
1. bake 1 tray at a time
2. bake for $\pu{9min}$
3. rotate
4. bake for $\pu{4min}$
5. cookies should be
	1. golden on edges
	2. pale golden on top
6. let cool on the tray before eating
