# Mundibussi

|Ingredient|Amount (?? cookies)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|sugar (icing)|500 g|
|walnuts|280 g|
|butter|200 g|
|chocolate|200 g|

## Recipe

### Dough
1. grate **walnuts**
2. melt **chocolate** in water-bath
3. grate **butter**
4. mix **butter** and **sugar (icing)**
5. whisk until foaming/fluffy
6. add **walnuts**
7. mix well
8. add **chocolate**
	1. should be liquid but not hot anymore
9. let rest until dough is firm

### Baking

> [Mundibussi](Mundibussi.md) are more dried than actually baked!
 
1. preheat oven to $120^\circ\mathrm{C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. place parchment paper on baking tray
3. form small ($\approx 2\,\mathrm{cm}$) balls from [Dough](#Dough)
3. poke a small dent into the balls using a wooden spoon
4. place baking tray in oven
5. bake/dry for $\approx 25\,\mathrm{min}$
	1. leave the oven slightly open so the moisture can escape
6. [Mundibussi](Mundibussi.md) are done when thy are a bit firmer
	1. inside can still be softer
