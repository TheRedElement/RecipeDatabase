# Striezel

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (wheat) |1000 g||
|milk |600 mL||
|raisin|70 g||
|butter|60 g||
|sugar|60 g||
|yeast (dry)|42 g|1 cube|
|salt|15 g||
|egg |1||

## Recipe

### Dough
1. mix **milk**, **egg**
2. add **flour (wheat)**, **yeast (dry)**
3. mix well
4. add **salt**, **sugar**, **butter** (room temperature), **raisins**
5. continue kneading until smooth, shiny dough emerges

### Rest 1
3. cover bowl with kitchen towel
4. let rest for $\approx\pu{30min}$
	1. until dough doubled in size

### Braiding
1. split into 6 equally large parts
2. roll into balls and then into strands
3. braid 3 strands each into 1 Striezel (2 total)
	1. you can also split into 12 parts and braid into 6-strand Striezels

### Rest 2
1. place parchment paper on baking tray
2. place braided Striezels on baking tray
3. let rest for another $\approx\pu{25min}$
	1. until about doubled in size

### Baking
2. brush with **milk**-**egg** mixture
3. drizzle with **sugar** pieces
4. bake at$\pu{180^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced) for $\approx\pu{25min}$
	1. until golden brown
5. brush with molten **butter**
