# Striezel

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (wheat) |1000 g||
|milk |620 mL||
|raisin|70 g||
|butter|60 g||
|sugar|60 g||
|yeast (dry)|42 g|1 cube; according to instructions|
|salt|15 g||
|egg |2||

## Recipe

### Dough
1. mix **flour (wheat)**, **yeast (dry)**, **salt**, **sugar**
2. add $\pu{600mL}$ **milk**, $1$ **egg**
3. mix well
4. add  **butter** (room temperature), **raisins**
5. continue kneading until smooth, shiny dough emerges

### Rest 1
3. cover bowl with kitchen towel
4. let rest for $\approx\pu{45min}$

### Braiding
1. split into 12 equally large parts
	1. $\approx\pu{140g}$ for this recipe
2. roll into balls
	1. cup hands above dough-portion
	2. execute circular motion until smooth ball emerges
3. transform balls into strands
	> repeat until wished length reached (I do it 3 times)
	> 1. dough will try to contract
	> 2. repeating gives it time to settle thus, being ready for being extended a little more
	1. roll back and forth, while softly moving outward with your hands
		1. stretches ball perpendicular to roll-direction
4. braid 6 strands each into 1 Striezel (2 total)
	1. stick ends together
	2. place glass filled with water on stuck together end
		1. ensures that the strands behave and don't twist while braiding
	3. lay strands out in sequence ($1$-$6$ from left to right)
	4. initialization
		1. place $6$  in upper left spot (across the strands and significantly above $1$)
		2. place $2$ in upper right spot (across the strands and significantly above $5$)
		3. place $1$ between $3$ and $4$
		4. place $6$ back down (next to $3$)
	5. repeat until no dough left
		1. take strand below the one that's in upper spot and place in upper spot on other side
		2. take strand that was previously in the upper spot and place in center of lower strands

### Rest 2
1. place parchment paper on baking tray
2. place braided Striezels on baking tray
3. let rest for another $\approx\pu{15min}$

### Milk-Egg Mixture
1. place $1$ **egg** and $\pu{20mL}$ **milk** in bowl
2. whisk until combined

### Baking
2. brush with [Milk-Egg Mixture](#Milk-Egg%20Mixture)
3. drizzle with **sugar** pieces
4. bake at$\pu{180^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced) for $\approx\pu{25min}$
	1. until golden brown
