## Recipe

### Sauce al ragu
1. chop **onions**, **garlic**, **leek** 
2. grate **carrot**, **parsnip** 
3. roughly chop **tomatoes** 
4. salt **tomatoes** 
5. add **oil (olive)** to pan/pot
6. roast **garlic** for short time 
7. add **tomatoes** 
8. roast and squeeze to liquefy 
9. season with **oregano**, **salt**, **basil** (Italian herbs) as you go 
10. once only small squishy tomato pieces left 
	1. transfer to separate container 
11. add **oil (olive)**, **onions** to same pot 
12. roast until glossy 
13. add **minced meat** 
14. roast until significant amount of liquid is drawn out from the **minced meat** 
15. now start stirring (make sure **minced meat** is cooked evenly) 
16. season with **salt**, **pepper**, **oregano**, **basil**, **thyme** (Italian herbs) as you go 
17. add **carrots**, **parsnip**, **leek** 
	1. you can also add other soup vegetables
18. mix well and continue roasting for a bit 
19. add **tomatoes** and mix well 
20. add **tomato paste**, **bay leaves** 
21. let simmer on low heat with lid on top 
22. if not quite liquid enough, add a bit of water or **tomato sauce**

* this is slightly different from [SauceAlRagu](SauceAlRagu.md)
* you can substitute the self-made tomato sauce with $\pu{140mL}$ of store-bought one
	* make sure to still add a few **tomatoes** in that case


### Bechamel sauce
1. see [bechamel sauce](./SauceBechamel.md)

### Baking
1. ideally let the [Lasagne](Lasagne.md) sit over night for even better taste
2. preheat oven to $\pu{180^\circ C}$ [Fan Grill](OvenSettings.md#Fan%20Grill)
3. grease casserole with **butter**
4. stack **lasagna-sheets** - [Sauce al ragu](#Sauce%20al%20ragu) - [Bechamel sauce](#Bechamel%20sauce) til nothing left
5. sprinkle with **parmesan** or **mozzarella**
6. bake by $\pu{180^\circ C}$ for $\approx \pu{30min}$


## Notes

1. one can replace minced meat with a vegan equivalent
