# Beef Rendang

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|coconut cream|600 mL|
|meat (beef)|500 g|
|rice|300 g|
|cloves|5|
|garlic|4|
|anise (star)|3|
|fennel seeds|2 g|
|galangal|2 g|
|ginger|2 g|
|lemongrass|2|
|chili paste (dried)|1 tbsp|
|cinnamon stick|1|
|cumin|1 tsp|
|onion|1|
|sugar|1 tsp|
|tamarind paste|1 tsp|
|cardamon|0 g|
|oil|0|
|salt|0|
|water|0|

## Recipe

### Spice Paste
1. blend **onion**, **garlic**, **ginger**, **galangal**, **tumeric**, **cumin**, **chili paste (dried)** until smooth
2. add splash of **water**
	1. don't add a lot, paste shall be thick and not watery

### The Stew
1. bruise **lemongrass**
2. cut **beef** into cubes
3. heat oil in *large* pot over medium heat
4. add **cinnamon stick**, **star anise**, **cloves**, **cardamom**
5. fry for $\approx \pu{1min}$
6. stir in the [Spice Paste](#Spice%20Paste)
	1. keep stirring to prevent burning
	2. stir until paste has dried and you can smell the spices
7. add **lemongrass**, **beef**
8. stir until **beef** is coated with [Spice Paste](#Spice%20Paste)
9. cook until liquid released from **beef** mostly evaporated
10. add **coconut cream**, **salt**, **tamarind paste**
11. stir well
12. set heat to medium-high and bring to a boil
13. once boiling, reduce to low heat
14. cover with lid and let simmer gently for $\ge \pu{2h}$
15. stir every $\approx \pu{min}$ scraping the bottom
16. add **water** if liquid dries up before **beef** is tender

### Final Reduction
1. once enough liquid has evaporated that the **oil** becomes visible (separated), stir continuously
2. while stirring, pull the **beef** apart with a fork
3. remove most of the separated **oil** from the pot
4. keep cooking until sauce is thick, dark brown and sticks to **beef**

## Notes
* [Beef Rendang](#Beef%20Rendang) is a dry curry
	* you should not have a saucy-gravy in the end
* there are pre-packaged mixes for the [Spice Paste](#Spice%20Paste) which you can use instead of making your own
* you can reduce the cooking time by using a pressure cooker
