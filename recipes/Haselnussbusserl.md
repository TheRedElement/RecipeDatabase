# Haselnussbusserl

|Ingredient|Amount (60 cookies)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|hazelnut meal|300 g|
|sugar|180 g|
|hazelnuts|100 g|
|oblaten|60|
|egg|4|
|lemon (bio)|1|
|cinnamon|3 g|
|salt|1 pinch|

## Recipe

### Dough

1. whisk **eggwhites** until stiff ([EggwhiteStiffness](../resources/EggwhiteStiffness.md))
2. in separate bowl
	1. mix **sugar**, **hazelnut meal**, **cinnamon**, **salt**
3. fold into **eggwhites** alongside juice of **lemon**
4. cover and let rest in fridge for $\approx 30\,\mathrm{min}$

### Baking
1. preheat oven to $130^\circ\mathrm{C}$
2. place little piles of [Dough](#Dough) onto **oblaten**
3. place one **hazelnut** on each cookie
4. bake for $\approx 10\,\mathrm{min}$
	1. [Haselnussbusserl](Haselnussbusserl.md) shall not get color
