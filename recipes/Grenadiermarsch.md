# Grenadiermarsch

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|
| :- | :- |
|potato |500 g|
|pasta |300 g|
|sausage (selch)|2|
|onion |1|
|marjoram |1 tsp|
|oil |0|
|parsley |1 tbsp|
|pepper |0|
|salt |0|

## Recipe
### Prep
1. boil **potato** in salted water
2. boil **sausage (selch)** in water
3. peel and chop **onion**
4. chop **parsley**
5. prepare [Pasta](Pasta.md)

### Combine
1. heat **oil** in big pot or saucepan
2. add **potato**, **pasta**, **sausage (selch)**
3. season with **salt**, **pepper**, **marjoram**
4. roast until **pasta** and **potato** slightly crunchy
5. serve garnished with **parsley**

## Notes
* often **sausage (selch)** gets substituted by remainders from [Osterjause](Osterjause.md)
