# Rösti

|Ingredient|Amount (1 Rösti)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|potatoes|5|
|onion|0.25|
|oil|-|
|salt||

## Recipe
1. slice **onion**
2. peel **potatoes**
3. using *kitchen towel*
	1. squeeze all the liquid out of the **potatoes**
4. **salt** **onion**
5. **salt** **potatoes**
6. heat **oil** in pan
	1. low to medium heat
7. add $\frac{2}{3}$ of the **potato**
	1. if using a smaller pan (one big [Roesti](Roesti.md))
		1. cover bottom of pan
			1. build base for the [Roesti](Roesti.md)
	2. if using larger pan (several small [Roesti](Roesti.md))
		1. form small [Laberl](../resources/Laberl.md)s out of **potatoes**
8. make small *well* in center of all [Roesti](Roesti.md)
9. add **onion** to *well*
10. cover with remaining **potatoes**
11. let sizzle for a few minutes
	1. until steam from below softens/cooks **potatoes** on top
12. using *spatula*
	1. tuck overarching pieces underneath to get dense, round shape
	2. continue until sides start to look fried
13. turn over
14. let roast until bottom is also crisp
