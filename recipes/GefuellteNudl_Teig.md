# Gefüllte Nudln

|Ingredient|Amount (16 pieces)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (wheat) |250 g|
|water |100 mL|
|egg |1|
|salt |0|

## Recipe

### Base

1. mix **flour**, **salt**, **water**, **egg**
2. knead until a smooth dough emerges
	1. dough should not be too firm
3. let rest for $\ge 1\,\mathrm{h}$

### Filling

1. take a small piece of dough
2. roll it out until it's about as thick as a knives back
	1. make sure the pieces are round-ish
	2. make sure the pieces are about the size of an average adults hand (palm + fingers)
3. add $\approx 1.5\,\mathrm{tbsp}$ filling i.e., [Kasnudl](Kasnudl.md), in the center
 1.using a spoon helps to keep your hands dry for [Krendln](#Krendln)
4. fold and squeeze the edges together
	1. squeeze until you get an $\approx 0.5\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide border of dough
5. apply [Krendln](#Krendln) to seal the edges

> you can also portion your dough first into the expected number of gefüllte nudl

### Cooking

1. boil salted water in pot
2. turn down heat so it doesn't wildly boil anymore
3. place fabricated dumplings/noodles into the water
4. let  sit for $\approx 10\,\mathrm{min}$

### Serving

1. heat **butter** until it starts to caramelise slightly
2. place finished dumplings/noodles onto plate
3. drizzle with the hot **butter**
4. garnish according to the filling

## Krendln

1. using thumb and the side of your index finger
	1. bend your knuckles
	2. place the dough edge on the side of the first joint of your index finger
	3. squeeze down and slightly pull outward with your thumb
	4. fold over (upward) the newly emerging edge with your thumb
	5. squeeze down and slightly pull outward with your thumb
	6. repeat until you went all the way to the other side

![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y-ctVo35Xxo)

> it is easier if your hands are not sticking to the dough (dry hands)
