# Risotto agli asparagi

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|
| :- | :- |
|water|1800 mL|
|rice|800 g|
|asparagus|500 g|
|parmesan|40 g|
|garlic|4 cloves|
|onion|1.5|
|soup  seasoning|-|
|oil|-|

## Recipe

### Prep

1. dice **garlic**, **onion**
2. cut tips off **asparagus**
3. slice **asparagus** spears into thin discs
4. prepare [vegetable broth](./soup_vegetables.md)
    1. alternatively dissolve **soup seasoning** in **water**
### Merge
1. heat **oil** in pan
2. add **onion**
3. add **garlic**
4. add **rice** into pan
5. fry until **rice** turns slightly brown
6. bit by bit add [vegetable broth](./soup_vegetables.md) until **rice** absorbed it all
	1. **rice** shall be nice and soft after the process
7. with the last ladle of [SoupVegetables](../resources/SoupVegetables.md)
	1. add **asparagus**
	2. season with **salt**, **pepper**
8. keep cooking until **asparagus** is cooked through
	1. can still be a bit crunchy if you prefer
9. turn down heat to low
10. add **parmesan** and mix until creamy
