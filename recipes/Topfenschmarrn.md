# Topfenschmarrn

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|curd |250 g|
|milk |100 mL|
|flour |80 g|
|butter |50 g|
|rasin |50 g|
|sugar |50 g|
|farina |40 g|
|egg |4|
|salt |1 pinch|

## Recipe

### Dough

1. separate **eggwhite** and **eggyolk**
2. beat **eggwhite** until stiff ([EggwhiteStiffness](../resources/EggwhiteStiffness.md))
3. mix **eggyolk**, **milk**, **curd**, **salt**, **sugar**, **rasins**
4. mix **flour**, **farina** and add to rest of the mixture
5. let sit for $\approx 15\,\mathrm{min}$
6. fold in **eggwhite**


### Baking

6. heat **butter** in pan
7. add [Dough](#Dough) and bake until golden-brown ($\approx 6\,\mathrm{min}$)
8. turn over and repeat
9. rip apart
10. add little bit of **butter**, **sugar** to pan (for caramellisation)
11. serve drizzled with **sugar (icing)**

## Notes
- typically served with [Apfelmus](Apfelmus.md)
