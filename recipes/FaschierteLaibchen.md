# Faschierte Laibchen

![](../gfx/PXL_20251018_014943388.jpg)

|Ingredient|Amount (4 portions)|
| :- | :- |
|garlic|6 cloves|
|egg|2|
|onion|2|
|minced meat|0.5 kg|
|breadcrumb|200 g|
|marjoram|-|
|nutmeg|-|
|oil|-|
|parsley|3 bunches|
|pepper|-|
|salt|-|
|thyme|3 bunches|

## Recipe
1. soften old **bread-roll**/**bredcrumb** in water (~15min)
    1. <font color=red>rinse well afterward</font>
1. chop **garlic** and **onion**, **parsley**
1. roast **onion** in pan
2. mix **minced meat** with **onion**, **garlic**, **breadcrumb**, **egg**, **salt**, **pepper**, **nutmeg**, **thyme**, **marjoram**, **parsley**
    1. until sticky “dough”
3. form flat laibchen (laberl) out of “dough”
4. slowly  roast laibchen (laberl) on both sides
