# Lebkuchen gewürz

|Ingredient|Amount (7 tsp)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|cinnamon |8 tsp|
|allspice |1 tsp|
|cardamon |1 tsp|
|clove powder |1 tsp|
|coriander powder |1 tsp|
|ginger powder |1 tsp|
|nutmeg |1 tsp|
|anise powder |0.5 tsp|
|mace |0.5 tsp|

## Recipe

1. mix ingredients
2. store in an airtight, dark container
	1. content shall stay _dry_

> ideally you find the raw (non-ground) forms of the ingredients and grind them yourself

## Notes
- stores well for $\approx 2\,\mathrm{months}$
	- however, the flavor will fade
- **mace** is optional
