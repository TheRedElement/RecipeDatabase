# Linzer Augen

|Ingredient|Amount (20 cookies)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour|300 g|
|butter|150 g|
|sugar (icing)|100 g|
|sourcream|15 g|1 tbsp|
|vanilla sugar|8 g|
|egg|1|
|lemon (bio)|1|
|baking powder|0 g|
|jam|0 g|enough for all cookies|

## Recipe

- same as [Butterkekse](Butterkekse.md) with the addition of spreading **jam** on the cookies
- essentially plain [Muerbteig](Muerbteig.md)

### Dough

1. grate/cut **butter**
2. grate zest of **lemon**
3. extract**egg yolk**
3. in a large bowl add **flour**, **butter**, **sugar (icing)**, **sourcream**, **vanilla sugar**, **egg yolk**, **baking powder**, **lemon zest**
4. quickly knead into dough
5. place in fridge for $\approx 1\,\mathrm{h}$
6. roll out into $\approx 2\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick dough
7. using cookie cutter
	1. cut out cookies in shapes you desire
	2. for half of the cut-out cookies, cut out smaller holes in the center

### Baking

1. preheat oven to $170\mathrm{^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. bake for $10\,\mathrm{min}$
3. let cool down a little bit
4. spread **jam** on bottom part of the cookies (part without hole)
5. stick top part on top (part with the hole)

## Notes
- the traditional way is to use apricot, or red-currant **jam**
	- ideally the **jam** you use has some sourness to it
