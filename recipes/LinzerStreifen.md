# Linzer streifen

|Ingredient|Amount (70 pieces)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|butter |250 g|
|flour |250 g|
|almond meal |200 g|
|sugar (icing) |150 g|
|jam (currant) |125 g|
|egg |2|
|baking powder |0.5 tsp|
|cinnamon |0.5 tsp|
|clove powder |0.5 tsp|
|salt |0 tsp|

## Recipe

### Dough

1. grate _cold_ **butter**
2. for $1$ egg: separate **egg white** and **egg yolk**
3. mix **egg yolk**, **butter**, (remaining) **egg**, **flour**, **almond meal**, **sugar (icing)**, **baking powder**, **cinnamon**, **clove powder**, **salt**
4. place in _cling-wrap_ and let rest in fridge for $\ge 1\,\mathrm{h}$

### Baking

1. preheat oven to $180\,\mathrm{^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)

#### Base
1. take $\frac{2}{3}$ of [Dough](#Dough)
	1. place on _parchment paper_
	2. roll until $\approx 3\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick
	3. place on _baking tray_
2. using _fork_
	1. poke holes into the rolled dough
3. bake for $15\,\mathrm{min}$
	1. on bottom oven rack

#### Center
> do in parallel to baking [Base](#Base)

1. heat **jam (currant)** in pot
2. once [Base](#Base) done baking
	1. spread remaining **egg white** on top
		1. to avoid that [Base](#Base) gets soggy when **jam (currant)** is on top
3. spread **jam (currant)** on [Base](#Base)

#### Top

1. roll remaining [Dough](#Dough) until $\approx 1\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick
2. cut in $\approx 1\,\mathrm{cm}$ wide stripes
3. place diagonally on baking tray (on top of [Base](#Base) + [Center](#Center))
	1. with distance of $\approx 1.5\,\mathrm{cm}$
4. bake once again for $20\,\mathrm{min}$
	1. on central oven rack

### Post baking

1. let cool completely
2. cut into stripes using a sharp knife

## Notes
- you can use **hazelnut flour** instead of **almond flour**
- optionally you can drizzle the result with **sugar (icing)**
