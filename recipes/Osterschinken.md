## Recipe

### Einsuren
- = brining

|Ingredient|Amount (per 1 kg meat)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|pork loin?|1 kg|
|nitrite curing salt|100 g|according to package instruction|
|sugar|20 g|
|water|1 L|
|pepper (whole)| ??? |1 brief|
|juniper (whole)| ??? |1 brief|
|coriander (whole)| ??? |0.5 brief|
|cumin (whole)| ??? |0.5 package|
|bay leaf|???|
|garlic|???|

#### Brine
1. cook water until boiling
2. add **salt**, **sugar**, **pepper**, **coriander**, **cumin**, **garlic**, **bay leaf**
3. let brine cool down completely

#### Brining
1. choose a suitable container
	1. made from
		1. food-compatible plastic
		2.  stone ware

> DO NOT USE STAINLESS STEEL AS THIS CAN OXIDIZE!

2. add **meat**
3. cover **meat** with [Brine](#Brine)
	1. **meat** has to be covered completely
		1. place i.e. a plate or rock on top
4. cover with lid or cloth
	1. shall **NOT** be airtight (meat and brine shall be able to breathe)
5. place in fridge (at $\le 8\mathrm{^\circ C}$)
6. let sit for $8\,\mathrm{d}$

> rule: the meat has to sit for $8\,\mathrm{d}$ per kilo of the heaviest piece of meat

#### Post brining

1. rinse the brined **meat** with water
2. place in *cold* water for $2\,\mathrm{h}$
	1. such that the salt-content can adjust
3. place in **fridge** for $1.6\,\mathrm{d}$ to rest

> rule: the rest has to take $\frac{t_\mathrm{brine}}{5}\,\mathrm{d}$, with $t_\mathrm{brine}$ being the amount of time the meat spends in the [Brine](#Brine)

### Selchen
- = curing/smoking

|Ingredient|Amount (per 1 kg meat)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|embroidery needle|1|
|kitchen yarn|?|

#### Watering

1. place **meat** back into the brining-container
2. cover with **water** and let sit for $\approx12\,\mathrm{h}$
	1. reduces saltiness to the desired taste

#### Drying

1. using kitchen-towel, dry up the surface
2. with an **embroidery needle**
	1. poke a hole through the thinnest surface
	2. thread **kitchen yarn** through the hole
3. hang **meat** to dry at the air at $\le 9\mathrm{^\circ C}$ for $\approx 1.5\,\mathrm{d}$

#### Cold smoking

1. has to happen at $15-25\mathrm{^\circ C}$
2. for the wood choose one of
	1. beech, fruitwoods, chestnut, walnut, oak, birch, alder
3. convert to *dry* wood shavings
4. repeat until color of the meat is #todo (typically $2-3\,\mathrm{d}$)
	1. ignite chosen wood + redirect smoke into smoking chamber
	2. let **meat** absorb the smoke for $\le 8\,\mathrm{h}$
	3. remove burned wood
	4. ventilate smoking chamber and let **meat** rest for $12\,\mathrm{h}$

#### Maturation

1. let the **meat** mature for another #todo $\,\mathrm{weeks}$ by drying at the air at $\le 25\mathrm{^\circ C}$
