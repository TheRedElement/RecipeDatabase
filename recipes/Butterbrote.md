# Butterbrote

|Ingredient|Amount (XX pieces)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour |200|
|butter |120|
|sugar |120|
|sugar (icing) |100|
|chocolate |60|
|almond meal |50|
|milk |44.361|
|egg |3|
|baking powder |0|

## Recipe

### Dough
1. grate **chocolate**
2. mix **flour**, **butter** (room temperature), **sugar**, **chocolate**, **almond meal***, **milk**, $1$ **egg**
3. knead into dough

### Eggyolk glazing
1. mix $2$ **eggyolks**, **sugar (icing)** until foaming

### Baking
1. preheat oven to $180^\circ\mathrm{C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. cut out round-ish cookies
	1. they should have the shape of an actual butter bread
3. bake at for $\approx 7\,\mathrm{min}$ #todo
	1. NOT TOO LONG
4. while still warm, glaze with [Eggyolk glazing](#Eggyolk%20glazing)
5. let dry before placing in cookie yar
