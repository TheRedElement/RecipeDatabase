# Marzipan

|Ingredient|Amount (500 g)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|sugar (icing)|350 g|
|almond meal|150 g|
|egg|1|
|lemon (bio) []|0.5|
|water|2 tbsp|

## Recipe
1. sift **sugar (icing)** and **almond meal** into large bowl
2. mix well
3. add
	1. yolk  of **egg**
	2. strained juice of **lemon**
	3. **water**
4. mix well until a paste-like (playdough-like) consistency emerges
5. continue kneading gently with hands until paste becomes smooth and pliable
6. roll into log
7. wrap in cling wrap to keep airtight until needed

## Notes
1. will keep in the fridge for $\approx 2\,\mathrm{Weeks}$
	1. [Marzipan](Marzipan.md) will firm up slightly
2. will keep frozen for $\approx 3\,\mathrm{Months}$
	1. defrosting
		1. leave in fridge overnight (keep wrapped)
3. you can substitute **lemon** with **orange**
4. variations
	1. substitute **almond meal** with
		1. other nuts
		2. apricot/peach kernels
