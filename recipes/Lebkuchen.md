# Lebkuchen

|Ingredient|Amount (20? pieces)|Alternative Units|
| :- | :- | :- |
|flour (rye) |300 g|
|honey |200 g|
|sugar |180 g|
|butter |60 g|
|milk |60 mL|
|water |30 mL|
|baking soda |10 g|
|cinnamon |4 tsp|
|egg |2|
|allspice |0.5 tsp|
|cardamom |0.5 tsp|
|clove powder |0.5 tsp|
|coriander powder |0.5 tsp|
|ginger powder |0.5 tsp|
|nutmeg |0.5 tsp|

|mace |0.25 tsp|

> you can substitute **cinnamon**, **mace**, **anise powder**, **nutmeg**, **ginger powder**, **coriander powder**, **clove powder**, **cardamom**, **allspice** with **gingerbread spice mixture**

## Recipe

### Dough

#### Dry

1. prepare [LebkuchenGewuerz](LebkuchenGewuerz.md)
	1. skip if you're using a premade spice mix
2. mix **flour (rye)**, **baking soda**
3. add **sugar**, [LebkuchenGewuerz](LebkuchenGewuerz.md)
4. grate **butter** and mix well

#### Wet
1. in a separate bowl
	1. mix **eggs**, **honey**, **water**

#### Merge
1. slowly mix [Wet](#Wet) into [Dry](#Dry)
2. knead to medium firm dough
3. let [Dough](#Dough) rest in a cool place overnight $\approx 10\,\mathrm{h}$

### Cookies

1. roll [Dough](#Dough) out on a floured surface until $\approx 5\,\mathrm{mm}$ thick
2. cut out cookies using cookie-cutters

### Baking

1. preheat oven to $180\mathrm{^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. place [Cookies](#Cookies) on baking tray
3. brush with **milk**
4. bake for $\approx 13\,\mathrm{min}$ on central rack

#### Prep
1. prepare [LebkuchenGewuerz](LebkuchenGewuerz.md)
2. chop **nuts**
3. in a pan roast $120\,\mathrm{g}$ of **sugar** until brown
4. infuse with **coffee**

#### Baking & dough
1. preheat oven to $180\mathrm{^\circ C}$ [Fan-Forced](OvenSettings.md#Fan-Forced)
2. in a bowl mix **flour**, **baking powder**
3. add [LebkuchenGewuerz](LebkuchenGewuerz.md), **dried fruits**, **sugar**, **coffee-sugar**
4. stir until not too viscous
5. place mass onto baking tray
6. bake quickly for $???\,\mathrm{min}$
7. cut in small pieces

## Notes
- add a slice of an **apple** to the cookie jar to make sure that the [Lebkuchen](Lebkuchen.md) stay soft for longer
- don't roll the [Lebkuchen](Lebkuchen.md) too thin
	- it will get hard while baking
